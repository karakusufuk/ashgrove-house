Terms

Ashgrove House is a fictional, concept country house hotel. These terms describe how a live Ashgrove House would work; no booking made on this site is real.

Stays

  • Nightly stays only. We do not offer day rooms or hourly rooms.
  • If you leave early, you pay for the full night.
  • Seisiún weekends run Friday and Saturday nights. Bank holiday weekends run for three nights.
  • On weekdays there are no events, only rooms.

Cancellation

  • Up to 14 days before arrival: full refund.
  • Up to 7 days before arrival: 50% refund.
  • Less than 7 days before arrival: no refund.

Evening tickets

An evening ticket (dinner and party, €75) lets you join a Seisiún night without staying in the house. Tickets are limited on each night, and some nights are closed to outside guests.

Restaurant and spa

The restaurant and the spa are open to guests who are not staying in the house. On event nights, dinner is for guests in the house and evening ticket holders only.

Prices

All prices are in euro and include VAT. Spa treatments are paid at the spa on the day.
